## What frameworks are



Frameworks are reusable design principles and “standard packages” that can be applied across many streets. Proposals apply these frameworks to a specific location (corridor, intersection, district). This separation keeps the site consistent: one shared logic, many local applications.



## Core frameworks



- **[Street Design by Context](/frameworks/street-design-by-context/)** — How design changes by street type: residential, mixed-use, commercial, and arterials.

- **[Pedestrian Street Design](/frameworks/pedestrian-street-design/)** — Crossings, speed management, visibility, and self-enforcing pedestrian priority.

- **[Transit Priority](/frameworks/transit-priority/)** — Bus lanes, signal priority, stop spacing, and reliability fundamentals.

- **[Bicycle Design](/frameworks/bicycle-design/)** — Protected routes, intersection conflict reduction, and predictable geometry.

- **[Opportunity Cost of Car-First Infrastructure](/frameworks/opportunity-cost/)** — Land use, safety, accessibility, and why cars are uniquely expensive in cities.

- **[Lakefront Trail Framework](/frameworks/lakefront-trail/)** — A system design for safe, high-capacity walking and cycling along the lakefront.
