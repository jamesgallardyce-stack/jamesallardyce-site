\## Summary



Property management succeeds when operations are predictable: clear resident communication, fast issue triage, clean documentation, and vendor coordination that closes the loop. I use standardized templates and trackers to reduce ambiguity, shorten response time, and protect residents, boards, and owners from preventable problems.



\## Scope



\- Resident communications (notices, reminders, policies)

\- Maintenance intake, triage, and follow-through

\- Vendor coordination and quote evaluation

\- Preventive maintenance planning

\- Meeting support and documentation

\- Incident documentation (sanitized sample format)

\## How I run the work



1\. Intake request → confirm details and access requirements  

2\. Triage → set priority and target response time  

3\. Assign vendor or internal action → confirm schedule  

4\. Update resident → document status changes  

5\. Close out → confirm outcome, cost, and notes for future prevention
