## Summary

This proposal applies a simple principle: streets should be designed for good people who sometimes make mistakes, because those mistakes can permanently harm pedestrians and cyclists. The goal is to reduce risk through speed management, clearer crossings, and design choices that make safe behavior the default.

## Design intent

- Reduce conflict points and simplify decision-making.
- Make crossings frequent, legible, and safe by default.
- Keep vehicle access functional while lowering the harm caused by momentary mistakes.

## Implementation approach

Start with paint-and-post pilots (quick build), measure outcomes, and iterate toward permanent materials once the design proves itself in practice.
