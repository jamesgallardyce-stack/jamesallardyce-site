Preventive maintenance reduces emergency calls and repeat failures by making work scheduled and predictable.



Typical categories:

\- Heating and cooling systems

\- Plumbing fixtures and shutoffs

\- Fire and life safety equipment

\- Elevator and access systems

\- Roof and drainage

\- Exterior lighting and security
