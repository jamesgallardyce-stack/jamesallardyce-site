This is a general reference. Final responsibility depends on governing documents and the specific situation.



\## Common association responsibilities

\- Building structure and exterior

\- Common areas and shared utilities

\- Main building systems serving multiple units

\- Roof, exterior drainage, and shared access systems



\## Common unit owner responsibilities

\- Fixtures and finishes inside the unit

\- Appliances and unit-level plumbing/electrical beyond shared systems

\- Interior doors and unit-specific components



\## When it is unclear

1\. Document the symptoms and location  

2\. Review governing documents  

3\. If needed, inspect to confirm whether the issue is unit-specific or shared  

4\. Communicate the determination and next steps in writing
