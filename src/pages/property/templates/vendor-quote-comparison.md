Use this structure to compare quotes consistently:



\- \*\*Scope clarity:\*\* Does it explicitly list what is included and excluded?

\- \*\*Materials and quality:\*\* Are materials specified (brand/model/grade) when relevant?

\- \*\*Schedule:\*\* Lead time, on-site duration, and constraints

\- \*\*Warranty:\*\* Labor and material coverage, duration, exclusions

\- \*\*Insurance and compliance:\*\* Proof of insurance, licensing if applicable, building requirements

\- \*\*Cost transparency:\*\* Line items, allowance assumptions, change order rules

\- \*\*Payment terms:\*\* Deposit, milestones, final payment triggers

\- \*\*Communication:\*\* Primary contact, update cadence, response time expectations
