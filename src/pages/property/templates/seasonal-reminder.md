\*\*Subject:\*\* Seasonal Building Reminder — \[Season / Month]



As conditions change, please help protect the building and reduce preventable maintenance issues:



\- \*\*Heating / temperature:\*\* Maintain a safe indoor temperature to prevent pipe freezing.

\- \*\*Windows and drafts:\*\* Keep windows closed during freezing weather; report drafts or leaks.

\- \*\*Water leaks:\*\* Report dripping faucets, ceiling stains, or damp drywall promptly.

\- \*\*Balconies and storage:\*\* Keep balcony drains clear; do not store items that can blow away.

\- \*\*Fire safety:\*\* Keep hallways clear and do not block exits.



If you notice a building issue, please send: location, photos (if possible), and the best time for access.
