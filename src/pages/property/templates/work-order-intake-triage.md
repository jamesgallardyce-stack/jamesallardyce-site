\## Intake (collect this information)



\- \*\*Requester name and unit:\*\*  

\- \*\*Issue location:\*\* \[Kitchen / bathroom / bedroom / common area]  

\- \*\*Description (plain language):\*\*  

\- \*\*When it started / frequency:\*\*  

\- \*\*Any safety risk?\*\* \[water leak, electrical smell, no heat, blocked exit]  

\- \*\*Photos or video:\*\* \[yes/no]  

\- \*\*Access constraints:\*\* pets, availability, lockbox, preferred entry times  

\- \*\*Impact:\*\* \[minor nuisance / prevents normal use / property damage risk]



\## Triage (set priority)



\- \*\*Urgent (same day):\*\* active water leak, electrical hazard, no heat in freezing weather, blocked exit

\- \*\*High (1–2 days):\*\* recurring leak signs, appliance failure affecting sanitation, door security issue

\- \*\*Normal (3–7 days):\*\* non-urgent repairs, cosmetic damage, adjustments



\## Assignment and follow-through



\- \*\*Assigned to:\*\* \[vendor / staff]  

\- \*\*Target date:\*\*  

\- \*\*Resident update sent:\*\* \[date/time]  

\- \*\*Completion confirmation:\*\* \[resident confirms / photo / vendor closeout]  

\- \*\*Notes for prevention:\*\* \[root cause, recurring risk, recommended preventive action]
