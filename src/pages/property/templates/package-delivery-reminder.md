\*\*Subject:\*\* Package and Delivery Reminder — \[Building / Address]



To keep common areas safe and accessible:



\- Do not block entrances, hallways, or stairwells with packages.

\- Use designated package areas if available.

\- For large deliveries, schedule during approved hours and coordinate elevator access if needed.

\- If a delivery requires entry, confirm instructions with the vendor in advance.



If you experience repeated delivery issues, share the carrier name, time window, and photos if available.
