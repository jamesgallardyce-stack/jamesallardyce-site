\## Incident report (sanitized template)



\*\*Date/time reported:\*\*  

\*\*Reported by:\*\* \[Resident / staff / vendor]  

\*\*Location:\*\* \[Common area / unit / exterior]  

\*\*Type:\*\* \[injury / water leak / property damage / security]  



\### Summary (one paragraph)

What happened, in plain language.



\### Conditions

\- Weather / lighting (if relevant)

\- Any visible hazards

\- Any equipment involved



\### Actions taken

\- Immediate safety actions

\- Notifications (emergency services, building contacts)

\- Repairs initiated



\### Evidence

\- Photos (yes/no)

\- Video (yes/no)

\- Witness statements (yes/no)



\### Follow-up

\- Corrective actions planned

\- Preventive recommendations

\- Date closed
